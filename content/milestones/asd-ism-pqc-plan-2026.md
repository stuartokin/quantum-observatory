The ASD's Planning for Post-Quantum Cryptography guidance sets end 2026 as the first of three milestones toward the 2030 cessation of traditional asymmetric cryptography. By this date, organisations should have a refined transition plan accounting for their security goals, risk tolerances, system dependencies, and data sensitivity.

ISM control ISM-2073 (introduced September 2025) adds a standing compliance obligation: a PQC transition plan must be developed, implemented and maintained. For Commonwealth entities this is a live requirement now. The 2026 milestone is a checkpoint on the path to the end-2028 commencement and end-2030 completion dates.

The ASD's LATICE framework (Locate, Assess, Triage, Implement, Communicate) provides the recommended structure for these plans.
