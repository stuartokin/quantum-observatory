QUARTET (QUantum Advantage-Ready Trapped-ion Exploration Testbed) uses Oxford Ionics' proprietary Electronic Qubit Control technology: everything needed to trap and control qubits is integrated onto a standard semiconductor chip made in commercial foundries, with no lasers used for qubit control. Oxford Ionics was acquired by IonQ for $1.08 billion in June 2025.

The NQCC funded the supply through its testbed programme in collaboration with Innovate UK. The stated purpose is developing and validating commercial use cases — applications research rather than hardware benchmarking. The system is described as field-upgradeable to Oxford Ionics' highest-performing configurations.

This is an announced pilot deployment at one site. No applications results have been published, and no independent performance measurements of the installed system have appeared.
