The 17 funded projects were selected through the Invention for Innovation Funding At the Speed of Translation (i4i FAST) Quantum Technology opportunity, which was open only to Higher Education Institutions or NHS organisations connected to one of the two NIHR co-funded quantum research hubs for healthcare: Q-BIOMED and QuSIT.

Selected projects include quantum imaging with undetected photons for breast cancer treatment decision support (Imperial College London), quantum sensing of neurophysiological signatures of early Alzheimer's disease (UCL), a compact quantum-enabled anterior segment OCT for home eye monitoring (UCL/Siloton), and measurement of immune cell response for rheumatoid arthritis therapy selection (Oxford).

All awards are early-stage. No device has cleared clinical evaluation. The programme's purpose is to establish whether the technologies can meet genuine clinical needs in a healthcare environment before attracting further public or private funding. This is a screening stage, not a deployment.
