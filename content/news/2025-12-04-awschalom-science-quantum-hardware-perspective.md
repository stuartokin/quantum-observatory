The paper provides the most systematic cross-platform readiness assessment from senior independent researchers published in this period. Its value is as a reference document rather than as new experimental evidence: it collects the field's current state across all major modalities and subjects them to a common framework.

The identification of fabrication yield, wiring density, and power management as binding constraints is substantive. Most QEC milestones to date have used tens to hundreds of qubits not because the codes require few qubits, but because building and wiring large cryogenic systems reliably remains an unsolved engineering problem. The paper makes this engineering constraint explicit and distinguishes it from algorithmic progress.

Noted as routine because it is a review, not an experiment, and because its conclusions largely restate what the field already acknowledges. Its use is in the clarity of the cross-platform comparison and the explicit TRL framing.
