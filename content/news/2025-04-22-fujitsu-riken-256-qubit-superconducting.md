The 256-qubit machine uses the same unit-cell design as the earlier 64-qubit system, which Fujitsu argues demonstrates architectural scalability. The system fits within the previously developed dilution refrigerator through new high-density thermal engineering. A roadmap to 1,000 qubits at a new Fujitsu Technology Park building is stated for 2026.

The device is notable as a Japanese national programme milestone — RIKEN is a significant programme under-represented on this board — but the announcement is a capability claim without a published technical substrate. Until gate fidelities and error rates are independently reported in a peer-reviewed paper, this remains a hardware announcement rather than a demonstrated result.
