News items land here.

A dated event, not a readiness claim. The board maps how close things are to
being real; a stream of announcements mixed into that would corrupt the map. So
news lives separately and points at the board through `about`, and at the
research behind it through `establishedBy`.

`establishedBy` is the interesting field. An announcement is usually the visible
end of work published months or years earlier, and tracing that link is what
separates this from a news reader.
