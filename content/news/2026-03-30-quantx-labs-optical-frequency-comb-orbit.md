An optical frequency comb acts as a gear ratio between optical and electronic time domains. Optical clocks oscillate hundreds of terahertz — too fast for any electronics to count directly. The comb converts those oscillations into countable microwave signals without losing timing precision.

The TEMPO.Space programme aims to deploy a complete optical atomic clock in orbit — the first of its kind. The March 2026 mission validated that the comb hardware survives and operates in the space environment (thermal cycling, radiation, vacuum), providing space heritage that reduces risk for the full TEMPO deployment planned later in the programme.

The commissioning announcement came in July 2026, four months after launch. The gap reflects the time needed for environmental validation testing rather than any operational issue.
