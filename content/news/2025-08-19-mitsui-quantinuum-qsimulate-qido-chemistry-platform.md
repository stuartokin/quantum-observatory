QIDO integrates QSimulate's QSP Reaction software, which uses state-of-the-art classical quantum chemistry methods, with Quantinuum's InQuanto platform, which provides access to quantum computing hardware and emulators. The quantum component targets problems where strongly correlated electrons make classical approximations least reliable.

Mitsui serves as the exclusive distributor in Japan and plans to expand to overseas markets. Beta testing involved Chugai Pharmaceutical (drug discovery), JSR (semiconductor materials), and Panasonic (energy materials).

This is an early-stage commercial product, not a published scientific result. No molecule has been identified that QIDO simulates beyond what classical computers can handle. The value proposition at this stage is accuracy on problems classical methods already solve, combined with a workflow that positions users for future quantum advantage when hardware improves.
