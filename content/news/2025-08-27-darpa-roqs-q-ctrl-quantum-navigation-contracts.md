Q-CTRL's Ironstone Opal product uses quantum magnetic navigation (mag-nav): quantum sensors measure Earth's magnetic field, and AI software matches the sensor readings to a pre-computed magnetic map to determine position without GPS. DARPA's RoQS programme was established specifically to accelerate quantum sensors beyond laboratory conditions onto real military platforms, including resistance to vibration and electromagnetic interference.

The company's technology is also being tested with commercial partners including Northrop Grumman and Airbus, per MIT Technology Review. The contract is for development and further validation, not deployment — fielding on operational platforms has not been announced.
