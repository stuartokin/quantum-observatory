HARLEQUIN is a quantum-classical hybrid: a grating magneto-optical trap (gMOT) cold-atom accelerometer provides quantum-precision measurements that are fused with a classical ring-laser gyroscope, MEMS accelerometer, and precision clock. The gMOT is a compact cold-atom source developed over more than a decade by Strathclyde and CPI TMD.

The sea trial was conducted aboard Trinity House Vessel Galatea during normal buoy-maintenance operations — not a dedicated scientific voyage. This matters: the system had to cope with real-world engine vibration, unpredictable motion and weather conditions without special infrastructure.

No navigation accuracy figure was published. The significance of this trial is that the system ran at all in those conditions, clearing the first of many engineering barriers between laboratory demonstration and operational deployment. A programme of upgrades and a second sea trial are planned for 2026. Future trials will also incorporate an optical clock and gravity gradiometer.
