EO 14413 covers three technology areas with distinct provisions.

**Quantum computing:** Agencies are directed to prioritise procurement of quantum computing resources for government use and to develop public-private partnerships. The National Quantum Strategy must be updated within 180 days with emphasis on commercialisation.

**Quantum sensing and networking (Section 5):** The Secretary of War is directed to identify at least three next-generation quantum sensor projects for fielding by September 2028. NASA is directed to develop a plan for civilian quantum sensing and networking in space applications. This provision was the direct authority cited when the DIU launched Farseer two days later.

**Export controls and supply chain (Section 6):** The Secretary of Commerce is directed to analyse quantum technology supply chains and align US export controls with allied governments. This is the provision most likely to produce concrete regulatory changes affecting quantum hardware vendors.

The EO builds on the National Quantum Initiative Act of 2018 and the research programme authorities that lapsed in 2023. Restoring those authorities would require Congressional action; the EO resets executive posture and directs agency budgets and priorities, but does not create new funding.
