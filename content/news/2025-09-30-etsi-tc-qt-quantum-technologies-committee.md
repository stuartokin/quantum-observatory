The TC QT's terms of reference explicitly target the European Quantum Communication Infrastructure (EuroQCI), the EU's planned continent-wide quantum network. The committee covers quantum communications, networking, sensing, satellite quantum communications, quantum random number generators and quantum security (including hardware vulnerability assessment and side-channel risk methodology).

ETSI is one of three bodies officially recognised by the EU as a European Standards Organisation. Standards it publishes carry formal weight in EU procurement and regulation. The first deliverables expected are technical reports and white papers — normative standards will follow as technologies mature.

This is a committee formation, not a published standard. The value of the item is prospective: TC QT is now the body that will produce the European quantum network standards, and its existence matters for anyone procuring or deploying quantum communications infrastructure in Europe.
