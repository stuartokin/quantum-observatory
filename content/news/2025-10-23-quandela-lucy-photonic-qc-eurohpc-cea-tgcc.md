Lucy is assembled with 80 per cent European-sourced components — cryogenic modules from attocube systems AG in Munich and photonic devices from Quandela's semiconductor pilot line in Palaiseau. The full system was assembled at Quandela's factory in Massy in twelve months.

This is the second quantum processor at TGCC: an earlier Quandela system, Belenos, was deployed in May 2025. The procurement model — EuroHPC Joint Undertaking buying sovereign quantum hardware for shared European research access — is distinct from the US national-lab model and worth tracking as a potential template for European quantum infrastructure policy.

The system has capacity to scale to 24 qubits by 2026 according to the vendor. No independent benchmark or published result exists yet.
