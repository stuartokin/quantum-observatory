Qunnect's Carina system acts as active noise-cancelling hardware for quantum signals, executing continuous real-time analysis and automated counter-phase shifts to preserve quantum entanglement fidelity across deployed metropolitan fibre without requiring dedicated cooling or laboratory environments.

The DARPA contract is associated with the Quantum Augmented Networks (QuANET) programme and Small Business Innovation Research (SBIR) initiatives. The funding advances the next generation of the polarisation compensation nodule — a component that has already been validated in field conditions.

This is not a research contract to demonstrate that entanglement distribution is possible; it is an engineering contract to improve a system that is already operating. The distinction matters for the board: this is the first DARPA investment in quantum networking hardware that is already deployed on commercial infrastructure across two continents.
