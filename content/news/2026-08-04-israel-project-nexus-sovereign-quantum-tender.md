Project Nexus is the first applied project under Government Decision 4255, Israel's national AI and quantum strategy. The tender was issued jointly by the Prime Minister's Office National AI Directorate and the Finance Ministry Accountant General's Department, and invites private companies and academic institutions to compete to build a domestically produced national quantum computing platform.

No technical specifications have been published: the tender does not name a hardware modality, qubit target, timeline, or project-specific budget. The NIS 5 billion (~$1.66 billion) figure cited in coverage is the annual budget for the broader national AI strategy, not an allocation to the quantum project. Israel's previous National Quantum Initiative had already directed approximately NIS 1.2 billion (~$400 million) toward quantum research and infrastructure.

This is a procurement announcement. No bidder has been selected and no machine is under construction. Officials acknowledged the project as a long-term undertaking. The tender does not advance readiness on any existing frontier item.
